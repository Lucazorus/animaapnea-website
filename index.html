Every hold has three phases.

Knowing where you are in a hold is what lets you relax through it. Anima's timer is built around these moments.

Easy phase

No discomfort yet. Oxygen is plentiful and the only job is to stay loose: jaw, shoulders, hands.

First contraction

Your diaphragm twitches on its own. It's rising CO₂, not a lack of oxygen. Tap the screen and Anima records the moment.

Struggle phase

Contractions come closer together. Training is about letting them pass without tensing up, and seeing that first tap arrive later over the weeks.

Tables built from your record.

Enter your best static hold and Anima builds your CO₂ and O₂ tables. Then it times every round for you with sound and haptic cues, so you never watch a clock.

  • CO₂ table: the hold stays the same, recovery gets shorter. Trains your tolerance to the urge to breathe.
  • O₂ table: recovery stays the same, holds get longer. Trains you to go further, safely.
  • Your own tables: change hold, recovery and round count whenever you want.
